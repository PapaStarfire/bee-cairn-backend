# Edition #51 Promotion: LinkedIn Post

**Register: OmiGaia / SUPERGRIEF (Canon Two).** Not Hermification.
The Herald is Hermification territory; promotion of the Herald is
public-facing Canon Two. No Hermit persona, no "loves," no jokes.

---

Three thresholds arrive in four days this week, and none of the
calendars involved consulted each other.

Yom Kippur begins at sundown on Sunday and ends at nightfall on Monday.
The September equinox follows on Tuesday evening, when the sun crosses
the celestial equator and the light and the dark come level.

Here is the part worth knowing.

The equinox is not the day of equal light and dark. The sun is a disc
rather than a point, and the atmosphere bends its light, so sunrise runs
early and sunset runs late. True balance, what astronomers call the
equilux, arrives several days afterward. No announcement. No ceremony.
Nothing is scheduled around it.

Balance does not arrive on the day it is announced.

I keep company with people in the season that opens when someone they
love crosses. A great many of them will walk into Monday expecting
something to resolve on schedule, because the calendar said so, and will
reach Tuesday feeling much as they felt on Sunday. The only conclusion
available to them in that moment is that they did it wrong.

They did not do it wrong.

The ceremony marks the crossing. It does not manufacture the balance.
One is instantaneous and one is slow, and they are not the same event.
The work of a threshold tends to land late, on no announced date,
usually while nobody is watching.

This is not a consolation I have draped over the sky. It is the sky's
own schedule, and grief keeps the same shape, because both are governed
by the physics of a system in motion rather than by our preference for
tidy squares on a calendar.

Edition #51 of The Hermit Hut Herald, The Hinge and The Hook, is out
today.

Rev. Rabbi Henry-Cameron Allen, OCP, ICGC

## NOTES

**Canon check.** No em dashes. No emojis. No wound-narrative vocabulary:
no loss, closure, recovery, healing, grief journey, moving on, passed
away, lost loved one. "Crosses" used for the transition. No AI-tell
vocabulary. No Hermit persona and no first-person Hermit voice. No
prescriptive directives; the reader is never told what to do or feel.

**Psychagogue posture.** The post opens a door and names a pattern. It
does not hand anyone an itinerary. "They did not do it wrong" is the
load-bearing line and it removes an obligation rather than adding one.

**Layer honesty.** The disc, the refraction and the equilux are Layer One,
settled astronomy, and are stated as fact. The reading laid on top is
named as a reading, not smuggled in as science.

**Length.** Around 320 words. Long for LinkedIn on purpose: the equilux
fact is the hook and it needs its full explanation to land. If a shorter
cut is wanted, the first four paragraphs plus "Balance does not arrive
on the day it is announced" plus the closing line stand alone at roughly
130 words.
