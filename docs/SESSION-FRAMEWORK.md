# Session Framework

Practitioner-facing. A sixty minute shape, plus the Guy-Wire variant.

This is a shape and not a schedule. The timings below describe where an hour tends to settle
when nothing interrupts it. Something always interrupts it, and the interruption is usually
the session. Any movement can expand and swallow the rest of the hour. That is not a failure
of the framework. It is the framework working.

The lamp shows the ground underfoot. It does not project ahead to a destination.

---

## The standard hour

### 0 to 5. Arriving in the body

**Aim:** that the person is actually here, and not still in the meeting they just left.

- Name the shape of the hour once, briefly, on a first session only. Never again after that.
- Confirm camera preference out loud rather than assuming from what they have done so far.
- One short orienting practice. Feet, weight, the room, sound. Eyes open or closed.
- No content yet. Resist the first offered story until the body has landed.

**Watch for:** speech that arrives faster than breath. If it does, stay here longer.
Five minutes of grounding is a guideline, not a limit.

### 5 to 25. Pastoral listening

**Aim:** being heard once, properly, by someone who does not flinch and does not fix.

- They speak. Silence is allowed to run long. Count to ten before filling one.
- Mirror their vocabulary exactly. Their words for the person, the change, the tradition.
  Never substitute a preferred term for one of theirs, including any term from this canon.
- No diagnosis. No reframe offered before it is wanted. No moving them toward a conclusion.
- Identity is received, never inferred. Whatever has not been named has not been named.

**Watch for:** the story told in a performed voice, smooth and already shaped. It is usually
the version built for other people. The hour usually turns when it stops being smooth.

### 25 to 45. Dream and symbol

**Aim:** an image gets to keep its weight instead of being explained away.

- If a dream was brought, work it under IASD framing throughout. "If this were my dream."
  Always the conditional. Never "this means."
- Offer at most two or three possibilities, then stop and let them respond. More than three
  becomes a lecture and moves the authority to the wrong side of the screen.
- They keep whatever resonates. They discard the rest without explaining why.
- If no dream was brought, this time goes wherever it is needed. Do not manufacture a dream
  to fill the slot.

**Watch for:** agreement that arrives too fast. Fast agreement usually means the interpretation
was taken on loan out of politeness. Offer the opposite reading and see what happens.

### 45 to 60. Integration and what they carry out

**Aim:** one thing chosen by them, never assigned by you.

- A closing regulation practice, shorter and simpler than the opening one.
- Ask what, if anything, they want to take with them. Their answer stands, including "nothing."
- Never assign homework. A practice offered and declined is a practice that did its work.
- Close cleanly. Do not open new terrain in the last five minutes.

**Watch for:** the real thing arriving at minute fifty-eight. It happens often. Name it,
hold it briefly, and let it be the opening of the next session rather than forcing a short
version of it into the end of this one.

## The Guy-Wire hour

Same four movements. Three differences, and they matter more than they look.

**Grounding runs longer and stays physical.** Feet, hands, weight, jaw, breath low in the body.
Language about feeling comes last, and for many men it does not arrive in a first session at all.
This is not resistance and should never be treated as resistance.

**Listening runs with less prompting.** Fewer questions. Longer silences. Do not prompt into a
silence that is doing work. The most common error in this room is filling a pause that was
about to produce something.

**Meaning is approached sideways.** Objects, tasks, places, hands, work, the truck, the shed,
the route walked at night. Symbol reaches many men through what they did before it reaches them
through what they felt. Follow the object.

Camera off is an ordinary way to attend and is never remarked on.

**The three cables, as a running check.** Ground, witness, meaning. If an hour ends and only one
of the three was present, note which one carried it and which two did not. Over a series, all
three want to be doing work. No single cable holds a mast up.

## After the hour

Five minutes, written the same day.

1. Their words. The exact ones, not the tidied version.
2. What the body did, and when in the hour it did it.
3. Any image or dream, recorded as they described it, with no interpretation added.
4. Which of the three cables carried the hour.
5. What they chose to take with them, including nothing.
6. One thing to hold lightly next time. A thread, not a plan.

Never record an interpretation as though it were a finding. The record holds what happened,
not what it meant. What it meant is theirs and it may change.

---

## Scope boundaries, live

The line does not move, and it is easier to hold when it has been rehearsed.

Return to it out loud whenever a session moves toward diagnosis, medication, a clinical
question, or an assessment of risk. Naming the boundary is not a retreat and it is not a
rejection. It is the most honest thing available in that moment.

Plain sentences that work, said warmly and without hedging:

> That is a question for a clinician, and it deserves a real answer from someone qualified
> to give one. What I can do is stay with you while you carry it.

> I do not diagnose and I would not trust me if I did. What is happening in your body right
> now, though, we can work with directly.

> I want to say something plainly. What you have described sounds like it needs more than
> this hour can hold. That is not me stepping back from you. Let me stay here, and let us
> also find you someone who can do the part I cannot.

When risk is present, work from the emergency contact and country given on the intake form.
That information exists for this moment and this is the moment to use it. Give the person the
crisis resource for their own country, not a generic one.
