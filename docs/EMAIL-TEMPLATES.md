# Client Email Templates

Seven templates covering the full arc of a booking. Written in Canon Two throughout.

Merge tags follow the Cal.com and Tally convention of double braces. Rename them to match
whichever tool sends the message.

`{{name}}` `{{date_time_local}}` `{{video_link}}` `{{intake_link}}` `{{booking_link}}` `{{duration}}`

Two rules hold across every template below.

**Nothing is an instruction.** Preparation is offered, never assigned. A person who arrives
having done none of it has prepared correctly.

**No message implies a wrong way to be.** Nobody is told to be rested, ready, open,
or willing to talk.

## 3. Before your session

**Subject:** Before {{date_time_local}}

Hello {{name}},

We meet tomorrow at {{date_time_local}}. Your link is here: {{video_link}}

Nothing below is required. It is offered because people often ask what helps, and these are
the things that tend to.

**A place where you will not be interrupted.** It does not need to be a good room.
People have taken these sessions in cars, in gardens, in stairwells, and in one memorable
case a laundry room, which turned out to be the quietest place in the house.

**Headphones, if you have them.** They make silence feel less exposed.

**Water within reach.** Speaking about this is more tiring than people expect.

**Something to write on, if you want to keep anything.** Some people write nothing for months
and then everything at once.

**Camera on or off.** Both are ordinary. If you would rather not be seen, that is a complete
preference and it does not need explaining.

If you are bringing a dream, it helps to have whatever you remember written down before we
start, however fragmentary. Dreams thin out in daylight, and a single image is enough
to work with.

Nothing needs to be interesting. Nothing needs to be resolved. You can arrive with no idea
what you want to say and we will begin from there.

## 4. Before your session, Guy-Wire

**Subject:** Before {{date_time_local}}

{{name}},

Tomorrow at {{date_time_local}}. Link is here: {{video_link}}

A few plain things.

You do not have to have anything prepared. Most men arrive with nothing worked out and that
is the normal starting condition, not a poor one.

Camera off is a completely ordinary way to attend. Plenty of men do the first session that
way and some do every session that way. It is not a lesser version.

If you have not said any of this out loud before, you can start in the middle. You do not
have to build up to it, give background, or explain who anybody was before you get to what
you actually came to say.

Somewhere you will not be overheard helps more than anywhere comfortable does.

That is everything. See you tomorrow.

## 5. After your session

**Subject:** After today

Hello {{name}},

Thank you for today.

You may notice something settling over the next day or two, or you may notice nothing at all.
Both are ordinary. Sessions like this sometimes land hours later, and sometimes a week later,
usually while doing something entirely unrelated.

If you took one thing away today, it is yours to use or set down. Practices are not homework
and there is no version of this where you fall behind.

If anything opened that you want to keep hold of, writing it down within a day tends to keep
it available. If nothing did, nothing is wrong.

Booking again, when and if you want to: {{booking_link}}

And the standing note that belongs in every message: this is not an emergency service. If you
need someone tonight, a crisis line in your country will answer, and findahelpline.com lists
them for over 130 countries.

## Sending notes

- Send 1 immediately on booking, from Cal.com.
- Send 2 only when the intake form has not been received, roughly 48 hours ahead.
- Send 3 or 4 around 24 hours ahead. Which one is decided by the strand field on the intake form.
- Send 5 the day after, not the same evening.
- Send 7 within a few hours, never the next day. The same day reads as concern.
  The next day reads as an invoice.
