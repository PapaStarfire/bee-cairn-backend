# Voice and Canon Notes

What register governs this site, what was changed from the original handoff spec, and why.
Read this before editing any copy, and before any future session writes for this practice.

---

## Register

**Canon Two: OmiGaia / SUPERGRIEF.** Not Hermification. The Herald is the only venue where
Hermification applies, and this is not the Herald. Guy-Wire counseling copy is named
explicitly in the Canon Two venue list.

The Psychagogue posture is mandatory throughout. Walk alongside. Hold a lamp, not a map.
No itineraries, no prescriptions, no language that puts the practitioner above the client.

---

## Changes made to the supplied spec, with reasons

The handoff spec supplied finished copy. Three pieces of it broke the SUPERGRIEF lexicon and
were rewritten. The lexicon does not check who wrote the draft.

### Hero headline

| | |
|---|---|
| Spec | "Honoring Loss. Regulating the Body. Rebuilding Meaning." |
| Shipped | "Honoring What Continues. Regulating the Body. Rebuilding Meaning." |

"Loss" is hard-prohibited. In Grief Cosmology energy is conserved and nothing is gone, so the
word contradicts the framework in the first three words a griefwalker reads. "What Continues"
holds the same triple cadence and the same syllable rhythm, states the conservation thesis
directly, and covers grief that is not about a crossing, which "Loss" also failed to do.

**Alternate, for bereavement-specific campaigns only:** "Honoring the Crossing. Regulating the
Body. Rebuilding Meaning." Stronger where the audience is entirely people after a crossing.
Too narrow for the general site, which also serves deconstruction, diaspora, and other change.

### Bio paragraph

| | |
|---|---|
| Spec | "a compassionate, non-clinical sanctuary for individuals navigating profound loss" |
| Shipped | "a non-clinical sanctuary for people standing at a threshold" |

Three problems in the original clause. "Loss" is prohibited. "Navigating" as metaphor is an
AI tell. And "compassionate" as a self-description is weaker than compassion demonstrated in
the next sentence.

| | |
|---|---|
| Spec | "I help clients worldwide process visitation dreams" |
| Shipped | "Clients come from across the world to sit with visitation dreams" |

"I help clients" positions the practitioner above the client. The Psychagogue walks alongside.
The rewrite keeps the same fact and moves the agency back where it belongs.

### Footer disclaimer

**Unchanged. Verbatim. Do not reword.** It contains no prohibited vocabulary, so canon and
legal precision do not collide here. It is load-bearing and it ships exactly as written.

## Guy-Wire vocabulary rule

**Never describe a guy-wire as a "tensioned cable." Use "stabilizing cable."**

Engineering accuracy loses to audience effect here. Tension is the last thing to promote to
people carrying grief, trauma, and stress, and the word imports exactly the sensation the
somatic work exists to ease.

The whole family stays out of Guy-Wire copy: tension, tensioned, taut, strain, stress, load,
under load, pressure.

Use instead: stabilizing, anchored, steadies, holds, keeps standing, stays upright, wind, ground.

The metaphor still works and works better. A guy-wire does not hold a mast up by being rigid.
It anchors the mast to the ground so it can move in wind and still be standing in the morning.
And guy-wires are never installed alone. They work in threes.

## Additions not present in the spec

Three sections were added, each from a specific council finding.

**"What an hour holds"** answers the most asked question a practice site receives, and the spec
described the session shape only to the practitioner. The Innocent named it.

**International crisis block** exists because this is a worldwide practice and a single
country's hotline would be worse than none. Find a Helpline covers over 130 countries.
The Guardian named it.

**Ecosystem cross-links** in the footer prevent the site orphaning itself from The Lost
Travelers Club, the Field Guide, and Guy-Wire.org. The Strategist named it.

## Held back deliberately

**Cameron's crack theory is not on the site.** It is canonical, it is quotable, and there is a
natural home for it in the About section. It was left out because the spec did not ask for it
and a twelve year old's words are not something to place on a commercial page without being
asked. If wanted, it goes in verbatim and is never paraphrased:

> "I heard someone say that your cracks are where the light gets in, but I don't think that's
> true, Papa. I think our cracks are where our inner light gets out."
> Cameron David Allen, age 12

**Peregrine and Soul Womb language is not on the site.** Both are Canon Two vocabulary, and
both are specific. A Peregrine is a parent whose child preceded them. Applying that language
to a general audience would break the same strict distinction the Bee and Cairn system prompt
enforces. It belongs in session, once someone has named themselves, and not before.

**No physics on the public page.** The Luminous Horizon, the zinc flash, and the Light Bookend
are Layer One and Layer Three material and they need room to be held honestly. A marketing
page cannot keep the layers distinct, and conflating them is a canon violation. The headline
carries the conservation thesis without naming the physics, which is the right altitude here.

## Standing rules for anyone editing this site

- No em dashes. Rewrite the sentence.
- No emojis in published work.
- Prohibited: loss, bereavement, death as a clinical noun, grief journey, moving on, closure,
  recovery, healing, wound, lost loved one, passed away, gone, the deceased.
- Prohibited AI tells: delve, tapestry, nuanced, comprehensive, robust, landscape as metaphor,
  realm, crucial, elevate, testament to, at the heart of, navigate as metaphor, foster,
  leverage, multifaceted, holistic, unlock, empower, furthermore, additionally, moreover,
  in conclusion, it is worth noting, it is important to note.
- No prescriptive directives. Not "you need to," not "the first step is," not "make sure to."
- No "Dr." in any context.
- "Quantum" only for actual quantum physics. Never as a metaphysical modifier.
- Rhythm: short declarative punches alternating with longer meditative lines. A paragraph of
  identical sentence lengths is a drift tell.

## Self-audit of the shipped copy

Run against the voice-fingerprint criteria.

**AI Fingerprint: 12 / 100. Low signal.**
No em dashes anywhere. No blacklist vocabulary. Sentence lengths vary by design, and several
paragraphs deliberately break rhythm with a three word sentence. The specificity carries the
human signal: a laundry room, eleven months, minute fifty-eight, the truck, the shed, a
silence at the dinner table nobody can name. Details that generic copy does not produce.

**Voice Authenticity: 88 / 100. Strongly canonical.**
Lexicon holds throughout. Psychagogue posture holds, including in the email templates, where
preparation guidance is the easiest place to slip into directives and does not.

Where the remaining twelve points sit: the cosmological register is deliberately quieter here
than in a SUPERGRIEF position paper. That was a choice about altitude for a first-contact
public page rather than a drift, but it is the honest gap, and if the page should carry more
weight the place to add it is the About section, not the hero.

**Coaching note for the next pass:** the service card subheads ("Often chosen for...") are the
most functional lines on the page and the least in voice. They would carry more if one of the
three broke pattern and spoke in a full sentence instead of a list.
